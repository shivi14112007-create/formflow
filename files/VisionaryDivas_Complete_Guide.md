## 🎮 COMPLETE QUIZ GUIDE — HOW TO RUN IT

---

### ACTIVITY 1: "WOULD YOU OR WAIT?" QUIZ

**Who runs it:** Shravani
**Time:** 6–9 minutes (3 minutes)
**How to run it:**

#### STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS:

**Step 1:** Shravani says:
> *"Okay everyone! Time for our first activity — Would You or Wait? I'll read out a situation. Type A, B or C in the chat as fast as you can! Ready? Here we go!"*

**Step 2:** Read each question slowly and clearly. Give 15–20 seconds for people to type.

**Step 3:** After responses come in, say the answer and WHY.

#### THE 6 QUESTIONS (Read these aloud):

---
**Q1:** "You notice the classroom projector is broken BEFORE class starts. What do you do?
A: Fix it yourself
B: Wait for the teacher to notice
C: Ignore it and sit down"

*(After responses)* "The answer is A! Fixing it yourself — without being told — is TAKING INITIATIVE. You saw a problem and acted immediately!"

---
**Q2:** "Your group project hasn't started yet and nobody is taking the lead. What do you do?
A: Start it yourself and get things going
B: Wait for someone else to begin
C: Complain that nobody is doing anything"

*(After responses)* "Answer: A! Stepping up when no one else does — that's what leaders do. That's initiative!"

---
**Q3:** "You see a junior student struggling with their assignment. What do you do?
A: Walk over and help them
B: Wait until they ask you for help
C: Walk away, it's not your problem"

*(After responses)* "Answer: A! You didn't wait to be asked. You acted with kindness and initiative — both at once!"

---
**Q4:** "A great idea hits you during a meeting but no one has mentioned it. What do you do?
A: Raise your hand and share it immediately
B: Keep it to yourself — what if it's wrong?
C: Tell a friend about it later"

*(After responses)* "Answer: A! Sharing your idea even when you feel unsure — that takes courage AND initiative. Don't let good ideas stay inside your head!"

---
**Q5:** "Your team is behind schedule on a project. Your part is already done. What do you do?
A: Ask who needs help and jump in
B: Relax — your job is finished
C: Wait for someone to assign you more work"

*(After responses)* "Answer: A! True initiative means thinking beyond just your task. You care about the team's success, not just your own!"

---
**Q6:** "You spot an error in a document your teacher shared with the class. What do you do?
A: Politely point it out to help the class
B: Stay quiet — it's not your place
C: Only tell your close friends, not the teacher"

*(After responses)* "Answer: A! Speaking up respectfully when something is wrong — that's initiative AND conscientiousness together!"

---

#### WRAP-UP (Shravani says):
> *"Amazing! Look at all your responses — so many of you already know what Taking Initiative looks like. The key is — next time you're in that situation, you actually DO option A! 🌟"*

### ACTIVITY 2: STORY TIME

**Who runs it:** All Members (each shares 30 seconds)
**Time:** 9–12 minutes (3 minutes)
**How to run it:**

**Step 1:** Samiksha introduces the activity:
> *"Now we each want to share a REAL story of when we took initiative. Then it's your turn — type your story in the chat! You have 1 minute."*

**Step 2:** Each member shares their story in 30 seconds:
- Shivangi: Festival decoration volunteer story
- Radhika: Google Doc notes story
- Samiksha: Covering for sick presenter story
- Shravani: Junior conflict resolution story
- Prarthana: Club social media story

**Step 3:** After your stories, Shravani says:
> *"Now it's YOUR turn! Type in the chat — one sentence: a time YOU took initiative. Don't overthink it — even small things count! Type now! ⬇️"*

**Step 4:** Read out 2–3 responses from the chat and celebrate them:
> *"Oh wow, [name] said [their response] — that is a PERFECT example of Taking Initiative! Amazing! 👏"*

---

### ACTIVITY 3: RAPID FIRE TRUE OR FALSE

**Who runs it:** Shravani
**Time:** 12–14 minutes (2 minutes)
**How to run it:**

**Step 1:** Shravani says:
> *"This one is FAST! I'll read a statement. Type TRUE or FALSE in the chat as quickly as you can. First correct answer gets a shoutout! Ready? GO!"*

**Step 2:** Read each statement, pause 10 seconds, then reveal answer:

| Statement | Answer | Say this after |
|---|---|---|
| "Waiting to be asked before helping someone is Taking Initiative." | **FALSE** | "Initiative means acting WITHOUT being asked!" |
| "Helping a classmate fix their mistake on your own is Taking Initiative." | **TRUE** | "You acted before anyone told you to — that's initiative!" |
| "Initiative means always doing everything alone, never asking for help." | **FALSE** | "You can take initiative AND still collaborate. They're not opposites!" |
| "Starting a task before the deadline reminder is Taking Initiative." | **TRUE** | "Being proactive — not reactive — that's initiative!" |
| "Fear of failure is a good reason to never take initiative." | **FALSE** | "Initiative is acting DESPITE fear. Perseverance helps you do it!" |
| "Pointing out a mistake in a meeting to help your team is initiative." | **TRUE** | "Speaking up constructively for your team = initiative + conscientiousness!" |

**Step 3:** After the last question, Shravani says:
> *"Wow you all are FAST! Great job everyone! I hope you're starting to see — initiative shows up everywhere in daily life. It's not just about big dramatic moments — it's in every small choice to act first."*

---

### ACTIVITY 4: INITIATIVE PLEDGE

**Who runs it:** Samiksha
**Time:** 14–16 minutes (2 minutes)
**How to run it:**

**Step 1:** Samiksha says:
> *"For our final activity, we're not just going to LEARN about initiative — we're going to COMMIT to it. I'll read the pledge out loud. Please say it along with me — or type it in the chat with your name and your specific action!"*

**Step 2:** Read the pledge slowly:
> *"I, [your name], promise to take initiative this week by [specific action], without waiting to be told, pushed, or reminded."*

**Step 3:** Say:
> *"Now type your pledge in the chat! Format: 'I, [name], will [action] this week!'
For example: 'I, Priya, will speak up first in my next group discussion this week!'"*

**Step 4:** Give 30–45 seconds for people to type. Then read out 3–4 pledges from the chat:
> *"Oh I love this! [Name] says they will [action]. [Name] says they will [action]. These are AMAZING! Please keep this promise to yourself — and remember it when you're about to wait instead of act!"*

**Step 5:** Samiksha closes:
> *"Remember — initiative is a HABIT. The more you practice it, the more natural it becomes. You've all just taken your first step — by showing up today and participating! We're proud of you 💜"*
